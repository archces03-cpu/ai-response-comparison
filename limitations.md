# Limitations

## 1. Small Dataset
This project contains only eight example comparisons. The results cannot represent the performance of AI models across all tasks or topics.

## 2. Manual Evaluation
The responses were reviewed manually by one evaluator. Different evaluators may assign different scores or prefer different responses.

## 3. Simplified Criteria
The rubric focuses on factual accuracy, relevance, instruction-following, and clarity. Other factors, such as safety, tone, completeness, and source quality, may also affect response quality.

## 4. Limited Fact-Checking
The examples demonstrate basic evaluation decisions but do not include a full reference-checking process for every factual claim.

## 5. Synthetic Demonstration Data
The examples are educational demonstrations, not results from a controlled experiment or a comprehensive benchmark.

## 6. Potential Improvements
Future versions could include more examples, independent evaluators, reference sources, additional scoring criteria, and agreement measurements between evaluators.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates a structured method for comparing AI responses. Its findings should be treated as illustrative rather than as evidence of general model performance.
